# Source notes for instructors

The demos are synthetic and intentionally simplified. They are derived from the training request and department scenarios:

- Historical documents and reference samples -> portable implementation.
- JEDEC-like or spec-like material + environment package -> coverage/sequence/vector-style flow.
- Database adhoc extraction -> wrapper-based query and analysis.
- Skill training material -> Query/API/CLI/report patterns, stop rules, AGENTS.md, opencode command, custom tool concepts.

Do not replace synthetic inputs with real manuals, internal specs, production data, or customer data unless they are approved and sanitized.
